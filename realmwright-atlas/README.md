# Realmwright Atlas

A whole Dungeons & Dragons 5e world in a single web page — for Dungeon Masters and players.

**▶ Play it here:** `https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/realmwright-atlas/`
*(replace `YOUR-GITHUB-NAME` with the account that owns this repository)*

## What it does

- **World map** — a 4,096 × 2,560-mile world grown from one number (the *seed*). Zoom from the whole world down to a 5-ft battle grid, with towns that have real street plans.
- **Live market** — 77 goods priced from the Player's Handbook, moving with season, local supply, wars, bandits, famine, plague and festivals as the calendar runs.
- **People** — every town has named residents with jobs, families, rivals, secrets and daily routines. Star anyone to track them.
- **Guilds & organisations** — the Adventurers' Guild, the Temple, the Watch, thieves and more, each with leaders, agendas and feuds. Quest boards that react to the world.
- **Monsters** — roaming groups and lairs drawn from the land they live in, with danger ratings, bounties and an encounter tracker.
- **Heroes** — a full character creator and play sheet (SRD 5.1 rules), tied to your hometown's market and people.

The full design notes are in [`docs/world-atlas.md`](docs/world-atlas.md).

## How to use it

1. Open the link above in any modern browser (phone, tablet or computer). Nothing to install.
2. Your edits, heroes and calendar save **in your own browser**. Clearing site data erases them.
3. To share a world or move it to another device: **World → Copy map code**, then paste it in on the other end.

Everyone who opens the link gets their own copy — you can't break anyone else's world.

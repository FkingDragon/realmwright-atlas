# Live table checklist (for a real session)

The automated tests can't sit two real people at two real devices, so check these at the table with Sean.
Tick each one and note anything odd.

## Before the session
- [ ] The DM opens the atlas and sees **Realmwright Atlas v25** in Help (the **?** button).
- [ ] The DM saves a **backup file** (World menu › Backup), just in case.

## Sharing the world
- [ ] The DM turns on live sharing (World menu › Share live). The chip on the map says it is live.
- [ ] Sean opens the same link on his own device and sees the same world, in **player mode**.
- [ ] Sean can't see the Monsters section, the editing tools, the thieves' guild or any DM notes.
- [ ] The DM renames a town. Within a few seconds Sean sees the new name.
- [ ] Sean makes a hero. The DM sees it under Heroes, marked "played by Sean".

## When things go wrong
- [ ] The DM turns off Wi-Fi for a minute and renames a place. The chip says **Offline · your changes will send when you reconnect**.
- [ ] Wi-Fi back on: within a minute Sean sees the rename, without anyone pressing anything.
- [ ] Only the DM can change the world, so open the DM's world on two devices (say a laptop and a phone) and rename the same town on both, a few seconds apart. The slower one gets a message saying whose change was kept.
- [ ] Close and reopen the page on both devices. Everything you changed is still there.

## In play
- [ ] Move the calendar on a week. Prices change in the Market, and the Chronicle tab of a town lists the days.
- [ ] Open a person whose relative has died recently. They are in mourning or visiting the grave, and their page lists the loss.
- [ ] On a phone: the map is on top, the atlas below, and the handle under the map gives either more room. Turn the phone sideways: the atlas goes beside the map.
- [ ] A 2024-rules hero at level 1: the Class step shows the 2024 features, and the Gear step shows the 2024 kit and its gold.

## Afterwards
- [ ] Note anything that felt slow, confusing or wrong, and send it over. It goes into the next version.

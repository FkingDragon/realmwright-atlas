World

Using the atlas

  1. Zoom with the mouse wheel, a pinch, or the ladder at bottom right. The world map becomes realms, then regions, then town streets, then a 5-ft battle grid.
  2. Explore: tap a town to see its population, trades, folk and local wildlife. Tap a road to see its length and travel time.
  3. Settle, Road, Paint and Label add your own places, routes, terrain and notes. Select something and drag it to move it.
  4. Market runs a live calendar. Every town prices real Player's Handbook goods, mounts and magic items, and wars, bandits, famine, plague, trade booms, festivals and feasts push prices around. Press Play to let time run.
  5. People shows each town's notable residents: jobs, families, rivalries, secrets, motivations and what they are doing today. Log the party's dealings with them and track the ones that matter.
  6. Guilds runs the Adventurers' Guild: quest boards in every town, NPC companies, and ranks from Copper to Adamantine for your party. It also shows how the town's organisations get on, and what its powerful people are pushing for.
  7. Heroes builds play-ready characters from the System Reference Document 5.1: race, class, background, scores, skills, gear bought at your hometown's live prices, and spells. The sheet rolls anything you tap and tracks hit points, slots, rests and level-ups; register a hero with the Adventurers' Guild to take quests. Tap Quick build for a complete level 1 character.
  8. Monsters shows creature groups living in their natural habitats, plus rare wanderers and haunts. They appear and move on over the weeks, while lairs stay for months. Place your own from the Bestiary (any Monster Manual creature, or a custom one), track each creature's hit points and initiative, and mark them slain. Monsters near a town raise its danger, which brings more monster quests, bigger rewards and higher bounties on the Market's Bounties tab.
  9. Links Underlined names go somewhere: towns, realms, people, organisations and events all open their own page and move the map to them. Press the open tool again, a tab you are already on, or the – button to fold the panel into a slim bar; tap the bar to bring it back.
  10. Measure gives distance and overland travel time between any two points.
  11. Everything saves in this browser. Use World → Copy map code for a backup. Undo with Ctrl+Z.

Shortcuts: V explore, K market, N people, G guilds, H heroes, B monsters, S settle, R road, P paint, L label, M measure, Delete removes the selection, Esc cancels. Character rules include material from the System Reference Document 5.1 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
